History

The History

icab stores all visited web pages in the History. The History will be ordered chronologically, so the page that was visited last will be listed first.

The History entries are directly accessible in the Navigation menu, but yu can also open the history in a window where you can search for history entries and also delete these.

Configuring the history

In the settings you can configure the history on the Bookmarks panel. You can define the maximum number of entries that should be stored in the history, and also the maximum number of days. If these limites are reached, iCab deletes the oldest entries.
